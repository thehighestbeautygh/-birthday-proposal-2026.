04 — THE PARTY SPACE

Warm at first.
Alive later.

Venue atmosphere reference
Replace with a venue photo when ready

Vine Restaurant, with After as an option to explore, could suit the lounge feel you're describing.

  • Ten tables of around five guests, subject to the floor plan.
  • Existing candlelight and warm lighting where possible.
  • Space for a bar, mingling, photos and dancing.
  • Finger foods rather than a formal plated-dinner setup.
  • Lighting shifts later in the evening as the DJ builds the energy.

Venue, exclusivity, capacity, minimum spend and activity permissions still need confirmation. This is a recommendation, not a confirmed booking.

05 — THE GAMES

Good people.
Good memories.

Simple activities that bring guests together without interrupting the party all night.

Games reference image
Upload IMG_4129.jpeg to the repository root
01

Who Knows Her Best?

Guests answer questions about her personality, favourite things and memorable moments. The highest-scoring guest wins a small prize.

02

Notes to Her Future Self

Each guest picks a card and writes her a kind message, wish or piece of advice for the year ahead. Collect the cards in a keepsake box.

03

Birthday Photo Challenge

Guests complete fun photo prompts throughout the evening and share their favourite moments.

04

The Final Face-Off

Two adult volunteers compete in a playful five-round challenge. Use non-alcoholic drinks rather than racing through alcoholic shots.

05

Make a Wish & Pop Your Balloons

She makes a wish, then everyone joins the countdown for a coordinated balloon-pop finale. Confirm cleanup and venue rules in advance.

06 — THE GIFT BOOTH

A little something.
A lot of meaning.

A LITTLE
SOMETHING
FOR YOU♡

A dedicated place for every guest to leave a gift, no matter how small.

  1. Guests leave their gift at the booth.
  2. They sign the gift register with their name and a gift number.
  3. A designated host keeps the gifts organised and secure.
  4. At the scheduled gift moment, the MC invites guests to present their gifts or acknowledges them together.
“Big or small, it's the thought that counts.”

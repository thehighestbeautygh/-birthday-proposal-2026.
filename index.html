01 / The concept

The Tailored Season: After Sunset

A celebration shaped by refined personal style, soft lighting and the shift from the ordinary into something more intimate. The look is polished but never forced, with room for individual expression.

Candlelit tables, warm tones, music and thoughtful details set the scene. As the evening unfolds, eye masks introduce a playful sense of mystery, giving the night its own distinctive moment.

The mood is elegant, relaxed and a little unexpected: tailored by style, transformed after sunset.

The celebration, from beginning to end

The evening, in order.

Every part of the evening has its own moment. Here's a proposed flow for The Tailored Season: After Sunset.

01

The beginning

Arrival & welcome

Guests arrive, greet one another and settle into the candlelit setting. Warm lighting, music and the bar help establish the atmosphere while everyone gets comfortable.

02

Food & conversation

Good bites, good company

Guests enjoy the skewers, sides, small chops and drinks. The photo booth is available for pictures, leaving time to mingle and enjoy the setting.

03

The birthday moment

Kwakyewah's special moment

Everyone gathers to celebrate Kwakyewah with birthday wishes and photographs. We can choose between a candle moment, a confetti reveal or another birthday surprise.

04

Let's play

Games & little memories

Who Knows Her Best? brings a little friendly competition. Guests can also write notes about what they appreciate about Kwakyewah and take part in the birthday photo challenge.

A note to her future self can be written and saved as a personal keepsake.

05

The switch

After Sunset: The Masked Moment

The eye masks and glow lights come out. The lighting dims, the music gets louder, and the atmosphere shifts from an intimate gathering into a lively, after-dark party.

Guests can dance, enjoy their drinks, take group pictures and let the night unfold.

06

The night continues

Music, dancing & photographs

The playlist takes centre stage while guests enjoy the bar, photo booth and company. This is the time for relaxed pictures, dancing and spontaneous moments.

07

One last surprise

The closing activity

The evening can close with a final fun activity, such as a balloon-pop challenge, if the venue permits it. The exact activity will be selected during planning.

This is a proposed programme. The final sequence and timing will depend on the chosen venue and the activities approved for the celebration.

01 / The idea

Dress well.
Stay for the night.

After Sunset is a dressed-up birthday party with room for everyone’s personal style. Tailored looks are welcome, but guests can wear any colour.

Later in the night, guests can put on eye masks for a fun masked moment. Think candlelit tables, music, a photo booth and small bites throughout the evening.

04 / Food & drinks

Food on
the menu

Small bites and sides, rather than a heavy main meal. These images are general references.

Skewer options

  • Grilled pork with roasted plantain and pineapple
  • Sausage skewers with potato fries, egg and pepper
  • Steak or other skewers with yam chips and watermelon

Small chops & snacks

  • Samosas
  • Spring rolls with spicy chicken sauce
  • Popcorn and other small snacks

Side options

Fries and other sides can be added to the final menu to make sure guests have enough to eat.

Cocktails

A cocktail menu with a few favourites and fun custom names. Long Island can be considered, alongside non-alcoholic options.

Food and drink options are suggestions. Menu, service and prices would be confirmed if the plan goes ahead.
